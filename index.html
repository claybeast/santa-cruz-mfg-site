Our Story

From The Shop Floor, Not A Catalog

Santa Cruz MFG is the rider-built parts line from Santa Cruz Metal Works, a working American metal shop that cuts, finishes and inspects every part it sells, right here in California.

The Black Widow started as a one-off for a personal build. Too many riders asked where to get one. So we tooled it up, dialed in the fit, and made it a product worth putting your name behind.

It's the first piece in the Webworks Series, a growing line of premium Harley hardware. Machined right, priced honest, backed by people who ride.

Questions

Good To Know

Will it fit my bike?

The Black Widow fits Harley Touring models from 2006 to current 2026 (Street Glide, Road Glide, Road King, Electra Glide, Ultra) and mounts with your factory hardware. Different bike or a custom seat mount? Message us and we'll work with you.

Do I need extra hardware?

No. The Black Widow mounts with the factory hardware already on your bike. Grab basic hand tools and bolt it on.

How hard is the install?

About 10 minutes with basic hand tools. No drilling, no cutting, no modifications to the bike.

When will it ship?

Orders ship within 2–3 days with free U.S. shipping. Once your invoice is paid, you'll get tracking as soon as it's on the way. Questions about your order? Email beck@santacruzmetalworks.com.

What finishes can I get?

Deep chrome or raw brushed aluminum today, with gloss black coming soon. Want something else? Custom powder coat colors are available, just tell us the color when you order. Every finish is built to hold up to weather and miles.
